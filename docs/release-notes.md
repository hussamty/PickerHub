PickerHub is an Arabic and English desktop color workbench for Linux, Windows, and macOS. Each archive includes its runtime.

## New in 1.2.0

- Compact view for busy pictures: when an image has more than five colors, the guide shows its five most dominant first. **Show all** brings back every color, and **Show top 5** returns to the compact view.
- **Save palette** and export take the colors on show, so a five-color palette is one click away.
- **Copy color code** in the color actions copies the color in your chosen format from image labels, sampled pixels and palette swatches.
- Includes 1.1.0's image color guide and perceptual color names: a muted or dark purple is never called gray.

## Downloads

- **Linux x64:** use `pickerhub_1.2.0_amd64.deb` on Ubuntu or Debian, or extract `PickerHub-1.2.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` on other supported distributions.
- **Windows x64:** extract `PickerHub-1.2.0-win-x64.zip` and open `PickerHub/PickerHub.exe`. Windows may show an unknown-publisher notice for this unsigned preview.
- **macOS:** extract `PickerHub-1.2.0-osx-arm64.zip` for Apple Silicon or `PickerHub-1.2.0-osx-x64.zip` for Intel. The preview bundles are ad-hoc signed and have not been notarized.
