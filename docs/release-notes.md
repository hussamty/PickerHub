PickerHub is an Arabic and English desktop color workbench for Linux, Windows, and macOS. Each archive includes its runtime.

## New in 1.1.0

- See an image's colors where they are. Each color gets a glass mark inside its own area of the picture and a leader out to a glass label with its name, code and share. Leaders never cross, labels never overlap, and they draw out one after another.
- Colors are named by how they look. One perceptual classifier (OKLCH) names every color in the app, so muted and dark purples are no longer called gray, tinted grays keep their tint, and purple, violet, magenta, blue and brown are told apart. Image colors are named from a robust median of each area, not thrown off by shadows, highlights or noise.
- A sampled pixel shows its name beside its code. Swatches saved by 1.0 that still carry their old automatic name are renamed once; names you typed are kept.

## Downloads

- **Linux x64:** use `pickerhub_1.1.0_amd64.deb` on Ubuntu or Debian, or extract `PickerHub-1.1.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` on other supported distributions.
- **Windows x64:** extract `PickerHub-1.1.0-win-x64.zip` and open `PickerHub/PickerHub.exe`. Windows may show an unknown-publisher notice for this unsigned preview.
- **macOS:** extract `PickerHub-1.1.0-osx-arm64.zip` for Apple Silicon or `PickerHub-1.1.0-osx-x64.zip` for Intel. The preview bundles are ad-hoc signed and have not been notarized.
